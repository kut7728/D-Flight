D-Flight Privacy Policy

D-Flight helps you check departure dates and airport information for saved flights and receive alerts. This policy describes the information the app handles and why.

Effective: October 16, 2026

Information we handle

  • Account information: user identifier and email address provided through Apple or Google sign-in, and a name if the provider shares it
  • Flight information: flight number, date, and airports entered for a search; saved flight number, route, departure date and time; and terminal or gate details entered manually
  • Settings: app and notification language, app notification and Live Activity preferences, and per-flight alert preferences
  • Device and app information: device tokens used for push notifications and Live Activities, app version, and service update times

We do not request or collect booking references, seat or passport details, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or payment information.

Purposes and flight searches

We use information to sign in and manage accounts, search for, save, and display flights, deliver requested alerts, protect the service from abuse, and respond to support requests. Flight searches are sent through our server to public flight-data APIs operated by Incheon International Airport Corporation or Korea Airports Corporation. D-Flight does not save searches as search history. Some flight results may be temporarily cached for up to 10 minutes without an account identifier to serve responses. Public API providers may process technical information about server requests.

Sharing and service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, server functions, and push notifications) and Apple and Google sign-in services to operate D-Flight. Data is primarily stored and processed in Google Cloud’s Seoul region; these providers may process data in other regions as part of operating their services. We do not sell data or use it for advertising tracking.

Manually entered flight information is stored to display that flight in the account. D-Flight does not currently offer a feature that shares user-submitted flight information with other users.

Retention and deletion

Account, settings, saved-flight, and device-token data are kept while the account is in use. When you request account deletion in Settings, the Firebase account, profile, device records, and saved flights are deleted. The author identifier on any existing flight data may be removed, and past flight data with no active trackers is eligible for cleanup after departure. Limited security and operational records may be retained as needed.

Your choices and contact

You can turn off notifications in the app settings and delete account data in Settings → Account → Delete Account. For privacy questions, contact kut.support@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I find a flight?
Availability depends on when flight information is published and what the public data providers cover. Check the flight number and date. If the schedule has not been published yet, use the app’s waitlist registration.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, choose Settings → Account → Delete Account.
